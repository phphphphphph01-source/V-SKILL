# V-SKILL Item Separation

Avatar Studio contains only:
- 10 companion avatars
- cosmetic accessories (head, backpack, accessory, visual effect)

Skill Items are intentionally NOT Avatar Studio cosmetics.
They remain in Mission/Quiz as contextual learning tools:
Hint Scanner, Knowledge Tablet, Logic Toolkit, Mission Compass,
AI Thinking Lens, Precision Lens, Time Booster, Focus Shield,
V-Buddy Assistant, Master Analyzer.

Cosmetic items change appearance only. Skill Items help reasoning without revealing answers.
